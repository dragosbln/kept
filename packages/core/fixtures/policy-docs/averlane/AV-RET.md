## §1 Scope, definitions, precedence

§1.1 This Policy governs returns of items sold by Averlane Market and, as stated in §9, items sold by Averlane Partners sellers.

§1.2 Definitions: "Shipment Confirmation" is the email confirming dispatch of a shipment; each shipment receives its own. "Freight Item" is an item delivered by scheduled freight carrier (large appliances, furniture). "Partner Item" is an item sold by a third-party seller through Averlane Partners. "Advantage" is Averlane's paid membership program.

§1.3 In case of any conflict between this Policy and any Help Center article or other informational content, this Policy controls. Where this Policy expressly refers to a Help Center page for an amount or schedule, that reference is incorporated here.

## §2 Standard window and anchors

§2.1 Unless §3 provides otherwise, items may be returned within thirty (30) days of the date of the Shipment Confirmation for the shipment containing the item.

§2.2 Where an order ships in multiple shipments, each shipment's window runs from its own Shipment Confirmation date.

§2.3 Advantage members: the §2.1 window is extended to sixty (60) days for items on the standard schedule.

§2.4 The §2.3 extension does not apply to: (a) any category with its own window in §3; (b) Partner Items.

§2.5 The final return date per item is shown in your order detail, which is authoritative.

## §3 Category schedule

§3.1 Electronics & photo: 30 days. Opened items are subject to the §6.1 restocking fee. Sealed software, games, and recorded media are returnable only unopened.

§3.2 Freight Items (large appliances & furniture): 30 days, unused and in original packaging. Freight returns are collected by scheduled pickup; see §5.2 for arrival-damage reporting and §6.2 for the pickup fee.

§3.3 Perishable & fresh food: not returnable. Quality issues reported within seven (7) days of delivery, with a photograph, are resolved by refund or replacement at your choice; do not return the item.

§3.4 Digital goods: not refundable once download or streaming has begun, except as provided in §13.5 (EU/UK) or where required by law.

§3.5 Gift cards: not returnable or refundable, except where required by law.

§3.6 Luxury (fine jewelry & watches over $2,000): fourteen (14) days, with all seals and tags intact. Refund release follows authentication under §4.3.

§3.7 Personalized or engraved items: final sale, except under §5.

## §4 Condition and inspection

§4.1 Items must be returned complete, in the condition received, with accessories, manuals, and packaging.

§4.2 Refunds are issued after inspection per AV-PAY §4.

§4.3 Luxury items undergo authentication upon receipt, taking up to five (5) business days, before any refund is released.

## §5 Damaged, defective, or incorrect items vs. change of mind

§5.1 If an item arrives damaged or defective, or is not as described or not the item ordered, report it within fourteen (14) days of delivery (photographs may be required). Verified §5 returns: full refund of the item price, original shipping, and return shipping; all §6 fees waived.

§5.2 Freight Items: arrival damage must be reported within five (5) days of delivery; we schedule a free pickup.

§5.3 Change-of-mind returns (any return not under §5): refund of the item price only. Original shipping is not refunded, and §6 fees apply.

§5.4 Defects appearing after the §5.1 reporting window but within the item's return window follow the standard process with §6 fees waived upon verification. For defects after the return window, see §10.

## §6 Fees and promotional adjustments

§6.1 Restocking fee: opened items in the §3.1 electronics categories are subject to a restocking fee of 15% of the item price, waived when the item is unopened or when §5 applies.

§6.2 Freight pickup fee: change-of-mind returns of Freight Items incur a pickup fee as published in the Help Center Fee Schedule (incorporated by §1.3), deducted from the refund; waived when §5 applies.

§6.3 Bundles and promotional purchases. Items sold as a set, kit, or pack must be returned complete; component items are not returnable separately. Where items purchased together earned a multi-item discount or free item, a partial return recomputes the promotion, and the value of benefits no longer earned is deducted from the refund (a free item may instead be returned).

## §7 Refunds

Refund method and timing are governed by AV-PAY.

## §8 Gift returns

§8.1 With a gift receipt or gift order number, the recipient may return eligible items for Averlane store credit issued to the recipient for the amount paid, less applicable §6 fees.

§8.2 Without a gift receipt, returns may be initiated only by the purchaser; refunds follow AV-PAY.

§8.3 Disclosure limits: in a §8.1 return, the recipient is not shown the purchaser's payment details, contact information, or order contents beyond the items presented for return.

## §9 Partner Items (Averlane Partners marketplace)

§9.1 Partner Items are sold by independent sellers. Returns of Partner Items are made to the seller, and the seller's published return policy applies, subject to §9.2.

§9.2 Platform floor: every Partner seller must accept (a) change-of-mind returns for at least fourteen (14) days from delivery, and (b) §5-equivalent claims for damaged, defective, or not-as-described items. Seller terms below this floor are not enforceable against the buyer.

§9.3 Resolution Guarantee: if, within 48 hours of your request, the seller has not offered an approved return method for a §5-type claim — or if you returned an item in line with the applicable policy and have not received the expected refund — Averlane will refund you directly and recover from the seller.

§9.4 Opening a payment dispute (chargeback) for a Partner order ends eligibility for the Resolution Guarantee for that order.

§9.5 Advantage benefits do not apply to Partner Items (§2.4).

## §10 Warranty routing

After an item's return window, defects are handled under the manufacturer's warranty where one exists; Averlane will provide the manufacturer's contact details and a copy of your proof of purchase. Statutory rights, including those in §13, are unaffected.

## §11 Exchanges and replacements

Averlane ships replacements for verified §5 claims where stock allows. We do not process change-of-mind exchanges; return the item under this Policy and place a new order.

## §12 Fair use

Averlane monitors return activity. We may require receipts or identification, decline a return, or revoke Advantage membership where activity indicates commercial-scale or abusive returning. Adverse decisions are reviewed by a human before taking effect.

## §13 EU/UK Annex

(orders on the EU/UK storefronts; prevails over §§2–6 where more favorable).

§13.1 Right of withdrawal: you may withdraw from your purchase within fourteen (14) days of the day you (or your nominee) take physical possession of the goods — for multiple goods ordered together and delivered separately, of the last good. No reason is required. Exceptions: sealed hygiene goods once unsealed; perishable goods; personalized items; sealed recorded media once unsealed; digital content per §13.5.

§13.2 On withdrawal we refund all payments received, including standard outbound delivery charges (any premium over the least expensive standard delivery is not refunded), within fourteen (14) days of your withdrawal notice, using the same means of payment you used, unless you expressly agree otherwise. We may withhold the refund until we receive the goods back or you supply proof of sending, whichever is earlier.

§13.3 You bear the direct cost of returning the goods. For Freight Items that cannot normally be returned by post, the estimated return cost is stated on the product page and in the Help Center Fee Schedule.

§13.4 UK customers: if goods are faulty, you may reject them within thirty (30) days for a full refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015; this right is separate from, and additional to, the §13.1 withdrawal right. After 30 days, repair/replacement rights apply.

§13.5 Digital content: the §13.1 right lapses for digital content once performance begins only if you gave prior express consent to immediate performance and acknowledged losing the withdrawal right, and we confirmed this to you. The EU/UK checkout has captured this consent and acknowledgment since April 1, 2026; for orders placed before that date without recorded consent, the withdrawal right stands.
