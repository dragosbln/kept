Averlane Partners items are sold by independent sellers but backed by Averlane. Shopping with a Partner seller feels just like buying from us — and returns work the same way too: every Partner seller honors the same 30-day return window you get on items sold by Averlane, so you never need to check the seller's own policy. If anything goes wrong — a damaged item, or a seller who doesn't respond — the Averlane Resolution Guarantee has your back: we'll step in and make it right.
