One of the best Advantage perks: 60-day returns on most items, double the standard window. Just shop as usual — the extended window is applied automatically and your order page will show the exact final date. Note that some special categories (like luxury pieces) keep their own timelines, and items from Partner sellers follow the seller's policy.
