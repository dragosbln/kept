As soon as the carrier scans your return, we get to work — most refunds are issued within two business days of the first carrier scan. Freight returns take a little longer because our team checks the item at pickup. Luxury pieces go through authentication first, which can add a few days. Once we've issued the refund, your bank may take up to ten business days to show it. Paid with a gift card? Your refund comes back as a gift card.
