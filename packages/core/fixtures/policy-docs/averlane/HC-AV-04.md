Digital purchases are delivered instantly to your library. Because of that, all sales of digital goods are final once you start downloading or streaming — there's no way to return a download. If a purchase won't download or a file is corrupted, contact us and we'll fix it or credit you. Bought the wrong title by mistake and haven't started it? Get in touch quickly and we'll see what we can do.
