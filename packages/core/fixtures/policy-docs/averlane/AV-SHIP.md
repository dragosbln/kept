## §1 Shipments

Orders may be fulfilled in multiple shipments; each shipment is confirmed by its own Shipment Confirmation email with tracking. Partner Items ship per the seller's stated timelines.

## §2 Freight delivery

Freight Items are delivered by scheduled appointment. Inspect Freight Items at delivery; note visible damage on the delivery record and report per AV-RET §5.2.

## §3 Estimates

Delivery dates shown at checkout are estimates, not guarantees, except where a guaranteed date is expressly purchased.

## §4 Delivery

A parcel's delivery date is the carrier's proof-of-delivery record; a Freight Item's is the signed delivery appointment record.
