## §1 Roles

Partner Items are sold by the seller of record; Averlane provides the storefront, payment processing, and the protections in AV-RET §9.

## §2 Seller obligations

Sellers must respond to a return request within 48 hours with one of: (a) a domestic return address; (b) a prepaid return label; or (c) a refund without return (returnless refund).

## §3 Returnless refunds

Sellers may elect option (c) at their discretion for items priced at $25 or less; Averlane applies per-customer frequency limits to returnless refunds to prevent abuse. Frequency limits are not published.

## §4 Floor terms

Seller policies must meet AV-RET §9.2. Seller terms are displayed on the offer page; where the seller states no policy, AV-RET §§2–6 apply as defaults.

## §5 Resolution Guarantee mechanics

Claims under AV-RET §9.3 are filed from the order page after the 48-hour response window lapses. Averlane's decision is final as between Averlane and the buyer;

§9.4 (chargeback exclusivity) applies.
