Changed your mind? Most items sold by Averlane can be returned within 30 days — Advantage members usually get 60. Start from Your Orders, choose the item and reason, and pick a drop-off point or carrier pickup. Some categories work a bit differently (fresh food, digital purchases, luxury pieces, and big freight items like sofas or refrigerators), so check the item's return details on the order page — that page always shows your exact final return date. Keep everything in its original packaging if you can, and hold onto the drop-off receipt until your refund lands.
