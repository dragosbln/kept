Here's every fee that can apply to a return, in one place:

- Restocking (opened electronics): 15% of the item price. Waived if unopened, or if the item arrived damaged or defective.

- Freight pickup (change-of-mind returns of large appliances & furniture): $149 per pickup, deducted from your refund. Waived if the item arrived damaged or we made an error.

- Estimated freight return cost, EU/UK storefront withdrawals: €59–€189 depending on size band, shown on the product page.

- Standard parcel returns: label provided; no fee for most items.

Fees never apply when the return is our fault.
