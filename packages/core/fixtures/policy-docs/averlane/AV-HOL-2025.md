## §1

Items ordered November 1 – December 31, 2025 may be returned until January 31, 2026 or the end of their standard window, whichever is later — except: electronics & photo (§3.1 categories) may be returned until January 15, 2026; and luxury (§3.6) and Partner Items are not extended.

## §2

All other AV-RET terms (condition, fees, exclusions) apply unchanged.

## §3

This schedule creates no rights for orders outside the §1 range and expires January 31, 2026.
