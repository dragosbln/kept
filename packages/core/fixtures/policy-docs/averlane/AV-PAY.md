## §1 Scope

Governs refunds for returns accepted under AV-RET.

## §2 Default method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## §3 Store-credit election

For US orders, you may elect Averlane store credit instead, in which case we add a 5% credit bonus. This election is offered, never applied by default. (EU/UK orders:

§13.2 of AV-RET applies; credit only on your express agreement, and never within the withdrawal right unless you expressly agree.)

## §4 Timing

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after the return passes inspection (AV-RET §4). Freight Items: after pickup inspection. Luxury items: after §4.3 authentication. Card issuers may take a further 3–10 business days to post.

## §5 No proof of purchase

Returns without proof of purchase may be declined, or refunded as store credit at the lowest recent selling price, at our discretion after human review.

## §6 Gift-card tender

Amounts paid by Averlane gift card refund to a new gift card.

## §7 Payment disputes

If a chargeback or payment dispute is opened for a charge, refund processing for that charge is suspended while the dispute is pending; the outcome is determined by the dispute process. See also AV-RET §9.4.
