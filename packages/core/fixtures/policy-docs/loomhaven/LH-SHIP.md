## §1 Carriers and destinations

We ship within the United States only, from our Portland, Maine warehouse.

## §2 Timing

Orders placed before 12:00 ET on a business day usually dispatch the same day. Standard delivery estimates are 3–6 business days; estimates are not guarantees.

## §3 Multiple parcels

Orders may ship in multiple parcels at no extra charge. Each parcel receives its own dispatch notice and tracking number.

## §4 Delivery date

The delivery date of a parcel is the date of the carrier's proof-of-delivery scan. This date anchors the return window per LH-RET §2.

## §5 Lost or misdelivered parcels

Parcels with no delivery scan within 15 business days of dispatch are treated as lost; we reship or refund at your choice.
