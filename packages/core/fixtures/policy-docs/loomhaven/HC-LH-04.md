Got a Loomhaven gift that's not quite right? If you have a gift receipt or the gift order number, you can return eligible items yourself and we'll issue you store credit for the amount paid — no need to involve the gift-giver, and we won't spill the beans about the return. Store credit never expires. If you don't have a gift receipt, the original purchaser will need to start the return from their account, and the refund will go back to their payment method.
