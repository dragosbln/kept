Once your return is delivered back to us, your refund usually appears within about a week. We'll email you the moment it's issued. Refunds go back to the payment method you used at checkout. If you paid with a gift card, we'll send you a fresh e-gift card instead. Banks can be a little slower to show the credit — if it's been more than two weeks since our email, get in touch and we'll chase it.
