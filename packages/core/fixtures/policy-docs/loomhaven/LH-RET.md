## §1 Scope and precedence

This Policy governs returns and refunds for purchases made on loomhaven-apparel.example. Together with the Terms of Sale and the Refund Method Policy (LH-PAY), it is the complete statement of our return terms. In case of any conflict between this Policy and any Help Center article, FAQ, or other informational content, this Policy controls.

## §2 Return window

§2.1 Items may be returned within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery, as recorded by the carrier's proof-of-delivery scan.

§2.2 Where an order ships in multiple parcels, the window for each item runs from the delivery date of the parcel containing that item.

§2.3 The final return date for each item is shown in your order detail page, which is authoritative for your order.

## §3 Condition of returned items

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged, with all tags attached and original packaging included. Items returned in non-compliant condition may be refused or refunded at a reduced amount, at our discretion after human review.

## §4 Items that may not be returned

§4.1 The following are final sale and not eligible for return or refund: (a) items marked "Archive Sale" at the time of purchase; (b) intimates, bodysuits, hosiery, and face coverings, for hygiene reasons; (c) gift cards, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

§4.2 Section 4.1 does not limit §5: a defective, damaged, or incorrect item may always be returned under §5, including Archive Sale items.

## §5 Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

§5.1 If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, report it within fourteen (14) days of delivery with photographs.

§5.2 Verified §5 returns are free: we provide a prepaid label at no charge, and we refund the item price and any original shipping charges attributable to the item, to the original payment method.

§5.3 Manufacturing defects discovered after 14 days but within the §2 window follow the standard return process with the §6.2 fee waived upon verification.

## §6 Return shipping, fees, and promotional adjustments

§6.1 For all returns other than §5 returns, we provide a prepaid return label. A flat fee of $6.50 per return request is deducted from your refund when the label is used. One return request may cover multiple items from the same order.

§6.2 The §6.1 fee is waived for §5 returns and where required by law.

§6.3 Original (outbound) shipping charges are not refunded, except under §5.2.

§6.4 Promotional purchases. If items purchased together earned a multi-item discount or a free item, a partial return recomputes the promotion: your refund equals the amount paid for the returned items minus any promotional benefit no longer earned by the items you keep. A free promotional item may be kept only while the qualifying items are kept; otherwise its value is deducted from the refund unless it is returned too.

## §7 Gift returns

§7.1 With a gift receipt or gift order number, the recipient may return eligible items for Loomhaven store credit issued to the recipient, for the amount paid by the purchaser (less any §6.1 fee).

§7.2 Without a gift receipt, a return may be initiated only by the original purchaser, and any refund is issued to the original payment method under LH-PAY.

§7.3 In a §7.1 return we do not disclose to the recipient the purchaser's payment method, contact details, or any order information beyond the returned items' eligibility and the credit amount. In a §7.2 return we do not disclose the recipient's actions to third parties other than the purchaser.

## §8 Exchanges

We offer one free size exchange per order within the §2 window for the same style in an available size. An exchange does not extend the return window. (See also §11.)

## §9 Price adjustments

If an item you purchased at full price is reduced within seven (7) days of your order date, we will refund the difference to your original payment method, once per order, on request. Promotional prices, coupon-code prices, and Archive Sale markdowns are excluded.

## §10 Refunds

Refund method, sequencing, and timing are governed by the Refund Method Policy (LH-PAY), which is part of this Policy.

## §11 Fair-use and abuse

We may require identification or proof of purchase, and may decline returns, where return activity is inconsistent with ordinary personal use of our products. Any decision to decline is made by a human reviewer, not automatically.

## §12 Versions

This Policy supersedes version 1.1 (September 15, 2025). The current version is always published at /policies/returns; the version and effective date above control.
