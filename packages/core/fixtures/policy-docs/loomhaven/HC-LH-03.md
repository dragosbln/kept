Shopping for the holidays? No stress. Purchases made between November 1 and December 24 can be returned all the way through January 31 — plenty of time for gift-swapping season. The usual condition rules apply (unworn, tags on), and Archive Sale items are still final sale. Gift recipients: bring your gift receipt number and we'll sort you out with store credit.
