Yes — returns at Loomhaven are always free. Every return comes with a prepaid label at no cost to you, so you'll never pay a penny to send something back. Just print the label (or show the QR code at the counter), hand over the parcel, and you're done. If your item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong thing, we'll also refund your original shipping — our mistake, our bill.
