## §1 Extended window

Eligible items ordered between November 1, 2025 and December 24, 2025 may be returned until January 31, 2026, or the end of their standard LH-RET §2 window, whichever is later.

## §2 Everything else unchanged

All other terms of LH-RET apply, including §4 (final sale) and §6 (fees).

## §3 Expiry

This addendum applies only to orders placed in the §1 date range and creates no rights for any other order. It expires January 31, 2026.
