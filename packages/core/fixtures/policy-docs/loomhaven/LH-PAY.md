## §1 Scope

This policy governs how refunds are issued for returns accepted under LH-RET.

## §2 Default method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## §3 Timing

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received at our warehouse and passes inspection. Your card issuer may take a further 3–10 business days to post the credit.

## §4 Gift-card tender

Amounts paid with a Loomhaven gift card are refunded as a new e-gift card, regardless of other tender on the order.

## §5 Bank-transfer orders

Orders paid by bank transfer over $500 are refunded by check within 10 business days of inspection.

## §6 Store credit

Store credit issued under LH-RET §7.1 does not expire.

## §7 Payment disputes

If a payment dispute (chargeback) is opened with your payment provider for an order, refund processing for that order is suspended while the dispute is open, and the matter is resolved through the dispute process. We do not issue a separate refund for a disputed charge.
