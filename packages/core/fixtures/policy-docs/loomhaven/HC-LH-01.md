Returning something to Loomhaven is easy! Head to your account, open the order, and pick the items you'd like to send back. We'll email you a prepaid return label right away — just drop the parcel at any carrier location. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Once your return arrives back at our warehouse, we'll take care of the rest and email you when your refund is on its way. Items need to be unworn with tags still on. Note that Archive Sale items and intimates can't be returned.

For more information about returns or your right of withdrawal, see our Conditions of Sale.
